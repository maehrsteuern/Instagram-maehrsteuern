## Topics that work in the US (no German tax law!)
Use German tax topics only as the personal story ("trained by the German tax office"). Tax content is **US tax**:
- **ASC 740 / tax provision**: hard-coded 21 %, broken links, no audit trail, rate reconciliation, deferred tax rollforward, SOX questions.
- **One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, signed July 4, 2025)** – always with fact-check:
  - § 163(j): adjusted taxable income is EBITDA-based again for tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2024 → bigger interest deduction.
  - § 174A: domestic research & experimental expenditures are deductible again for tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2024 (foreign R&E: still 15-year amortization) → deferred tax assets from 2022–2024 capitalization reverse.
  - 100 % bonus depreciation made permanent for property acquired after Jan 19, 2025.
- **Deadlines as hooks**: Oct 15 (extended individual returns), Jan 15 (Q4 estimated tax), Jan 31 (1099-NEC / W-2), Mar 15 (partnerships & S corps), Apr 15.
- **Sales tax economic nexus** (e.g. $100,000 in sales into a state – thresholds vary by state), state apportionment, 1099 vendor checks.
- **AI vs. code**: why a chatbot shouldn't do your tax math; where AI helps (reading, classifying, drafting) and where code must calculate.

Demo numbers (use exactly these so all posts match):
- **§ 163(j) example**: EBITDA $14,000,000 · D&A $4,000,000 · EBIT $10,000,000 · business interest expense $4,000,000. Old (EBIT): limit 30 % × $10.0M = $3.0M → $1.0M disallowed. New (EBITDA): 30 % × $14.0M = $4.2M → all $4.0M deductible. **+$1,000,000 deduction × 21 % = $210,000 less federal tax.**
- **§ 174A example**: $2,000,000 domestic R&E in 2025. Old § 174 (5 years, mid-year convention): year-1 deduction 10 % = $200,000. New § 174A: $2,000,000 now. **+$1,800,000 deduction × 21 % = $378,000 less federal tax in year 1.**
- **Provision example**: pre-tax income $8,400,000 · 21 % federal · 5 % state (net of federal benefit: 3.95 %) · demo company "Northwind Manufacturing Inc." (fictional).
