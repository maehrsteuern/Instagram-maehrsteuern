## How the algorithm ranks Reels
1. **Shares via DM ("sends")** = strongest signal for reach among **non-followers**.
2. **Watch time and completion rate** – especially how many get past the first 3 s and stay to the end (loops count as rewatches).
3. **Saves** work similarly to shares.
4. Likes and comments count mostly with **your own followers**, hardly for new reach.
- Process: Instagram first shows the Reel to a small test group, then to bigger groups, then to non-followers. It only moves on if engagement grows faster than the natural decay → **the first hour** matters (that's why Reels go out at 12:30 PM ET, when Loris can be online, see `05_publishing.md`).
- A single viral hit is not a strategy: reach only pays off if it turns into **follows and DMs** (profile, bio, pinned posts and the DM funnel must be in place).

## Build for shares and saves, not for likes
- Before every post ask: **"Who sends this to whom – and why?"** "Because it's good" isn't enough.
  - For us: the tax manager sends it to the coworker who owns the provision workbook; CPA candidates send it to each other; a CPA sends it to a small-business client.
- What gets shared: **a concrete, slightly contrarian take** ("Hard-coded tax rates are a SOX finding waiting to happen"), **checklists**, **comparisons**, **mistakes & myths** (aha moment), **satisfying before/after** (pillar 5).
- What gets saved: reference content (deadlines, thresholds, step-by-step checks, "7 things to prep for year-end").
- Last slide / end of the Reel: **one** clear CTA matching the goal – "Send this to the coworker with the 20-tab workbook" (share) or "Save this for your next close" (save). Next to it stays the DM CTA `TOOL` (`04_dm_funnel.md`).

## Hook – in addition to `08_hooks.md`
- **Write the hook first**, then the rest.
- **The thesis or the payoff is in second 1**, no run-up (no logo, no "Hey guys").
- **Say the thesis and show it at the same time** (Instagram measures watch time, not reading time) – fits our rule "subtitles word for word".
- **A face in the first frame** beats pure text or pure screen → format C "face + screen" (`10_software_reel_concept.md`) and "My story".
- Never hide the payoff behind second 10.

## Reel structure
- Mini story: **setup → tension → payoff** (matches `09_reel_rules.md`: situation → blow-up → turn).
- Length: the sources name **7–15 s** as the best range for pure demo/wow Reels (our formats A/B/S, 6–12 s). Story Reels with voice may be longer as long as completion holds → compare average watch time and skip rate per length in `06_analytics.md`.
- **Cover** with a readable title line – it stays in the profile grid.
- Safe zone: important content in the **upper ~65 %** (the UI covers the bottom) – our limit y ≈ 1500 fits.
- Always subtitles (many watch without sound – in the US often at work).

## Search: keywords, not just hashtags
- Instagram understands content via text and **spoken words**. The terms people search ("tax provision", "163(j)", "1099-NEC", "sales tax nexus", "ASC 740") belong **in the first caption line, on slide 1 and in the spoken text**.
- Hashtags: **3–5 relevant ones** are enough (`03_content_calendar.md`).

## Series and seasons
- **Series beat one-offs**: recurring title and look, e.g. "Spreadsheet mistake #1, #2, #3 …", "AI vs. code, part 2", "Satisfying software #4". People follow so they don't miss the next one.
- **Seasonality brings noticeably more reach.** For us the **US tax calendar** counts, not Valentine's Day:
  - Oct 15 (extended individual returns) · year-end provision & close (Nov–Jan) · Jan 15 (Q4 estimated tax) · Jan 31 (1099-NEC / W-2; Feb 1 in 2027 because Jan 31 is a Sunday) · Mar 15 (partnerships & S corps) · Apr 15 · 10-K season (Feb–Mar for calendar-year public companies) · law changes (OBBBA: § 163(j), § 174A, bonus depreciation).
  - US holidays reduce reach: Thanksgiving week, Dec 24 – Jan 1. Plan lighter weeks there.
- Strong format ideas that fit us: **tutorials** (short, usable right away), **mistakes & myths**, **before/after** (spreadsheet → tool), **satisfying loops**, **mini everyday moments** (10 s from busy season).

## Stories
- Unfiltered, behind the scenes; poll, question sticker, quiz (daily at 9:00 AM ET).
- Mini stories across several frames (before/after, "the night before the deadline").
- **Countdown sticker** before bigger posts or a tool release.
- No hard selling in stories; show the tool subtly.

## General
- Better post nothing than anything (quality over cadence) – but keep the cadence; content a few weeks ahead.
- Answer comments quickly; block time for it.
- No bought followers or reach: they bring "dead mailboxes" and distort the stats.
- Don't read stats by likes only: **shares, saves, completion rate, profile visits, follows** – and check that the right people are reached: **US share in "Top countries" and English-speaking tax/accounting people**. Watch out: German contacts from the sister account following @maehrtax inflate numbers but don't buy US services.

## Selling without cold outreach (YouTube video by Sebiforce on winning clients on Instagram, O47GvFx6VQ8, summarized via Gemini from transcript screenshots)
- **No cold DMs.** Mass messages with a sales intent annoy people and damage the brand, especially with tax pros. Our way: people write `TOOL` **on their own** (`04_dm_funnel.md`), content does the pre-selection.
- **Pull, not push:** show the offer, leave the decision to the client. Someone who voluntarily consumed several posts arrives in the DM already leaning towards a decision.
- **Roles of the formats:**
  - **Reels = reach** (new people to the profile),
  - **Carousels = trust and inquiries** (reach mostly existing followers; in the example ~4× more inquiries per 10,000 reached than Reels),
  - **Stories = closeness** (behind the scenes, unfinished work, your own opinion).
  → For the US: Reel-first (5/week) to build an audience from zero, but **carousels stay** (2/week) because they convert.
- **Trust takes months.** Lots of reach without trust brings no inquiries → keep the cadence, show your own opinion (brand voice), even if it costs some reach.
- **Evergreen content** builds a library that still works in years (step-by-step checks, typical mistakes, spreadsheet principles) – current topics (OBBBA) complement it, they don't replace it.
- **Emotional angle:** address the audience's fears and problems, show yourself as the solution ("The 3 mistakes that cost me hours in year-end close"). You don't have to be the smartest, just a few years ahead.
- **Sales phases in stories cost reach** (viewers drop off). Promote the tool in doses, never several days in a row – a 30–40 % drop is acceptable, more is not.
- **Freebie:** something free with real value that points to the tool. For the US this is live from day 1: *"The Spreadsheet-to-Code Checklist for Tax Teams"* via the TOOL keyword (`16_us_growth_playbook.md`).
- **Social proof:** real client stories beat feature lists. **Only with real, approved cases** (no invented results, no employer data).
- **Comment keyword with auto-DM** (ManyChat): live from day 1 (`04_dm_funnel.md`).

## Carousel or Reel in the evening (input Loris, 10/1/2026; numbers unverified, from AI research)
- **Professional topics in the evening → carousel** when it's about understanding, saving and inquiries: after work people have time to swipe and save tax/software knowledge for work. Cited: ~2× the saves, engagement rate ~7–10 % (Reels ~3–6 %). → Our carousels go out at 7:30 PM ET.
- **Second chance only for carousels:** if someone scrolls past, Instagram often shows the post again later – with slide 2 as the cover. → **Slide 2 must work on its own as an entry point.**
- **Reels → reach among non-followers** (cited: ~36 % more reach outside the community). A professional Reel must not be dry: strong hook in 1.5 s, mistake format ("Don't do this"), short.
- **Reel as teaser, carousel as depth:** the Reel points to the profile/carousel ("Full math in the carousel") or to the PDF via DM. Exactly our pattern carousel + companion Reel.
- Fits a carousel: step-by-step, code snippets, tax tables, 5–10 logical steps. Fits a Reel: quick tricks, mistake formats, industry insights, satisfying loops.
- Check against our own numbers in `06_analytics.md` after 4 weeks.

## Decisions for @maehrtax (brief, 10/4/2026)
- **Reel-first:** 5 Reels per week (Mon–Fri 12:30 PM ET), 2 carousels (Tue/Thu 7:30 PM ET), 1 story per day.
- **Satisfying software** as a recurring format (pillar 5), at least one per Content Factory run.
- **Hard conversion funnel:** every Reel hook ≤ 1.5 s, visible transformation, CTA; ManyChat + lead magnet from day 1.
- **Paid boost from week 3:** the best Reel by (saves + shares) / reach becomes a click-to-DM ad, USD 5/day, US only. Loris pays and submits himself.

## Deliberately not adopted
- Trends and trending sounds **without a link to the niche** – only if they translate cleanly to tax.
- Fast cuts and flashy effects as a default – too hectic for software (`09_reel_rules.md`).
- Instagram Shopping, location hashtags, employee/team intros, client stories without real approved cases.
- Fixed "best times" from lists – we use our own stats after 4 weeks.
