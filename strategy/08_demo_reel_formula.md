# 8 · Formula for demo Reels (8–12 s)

Learned on the German sister account (insights 9/30/2026): on its first demo Reel 68 % swiped away immediately, average watch time was 7 s. For the US the Reels are shorter and harder: hook ≤ 1.5 s, one visible transformation, CTA on screen.
Template to copy: the cut lists in `templates/system/cuts/` (e.g. `p02_reel_split.json`).

| Time | Building block | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.3 s | **Pain** | Real chaos image (spreadsheet, `#REF!`, red cells) + a hook that hits a known pain, not a statement. Text from the first frame (instant-text option in the cut list). |
| 1.3–3.4 s | **Cut + open loop** | Hard cut to the tool. Subline promises what's coming ("Watch the status light."). |
| 3.4–5.4 s | **1 benefit** | One bar, max. 2 s. Better one claim less. |
| 5.4–8.6 s | **Payoff** | Visible change (red → yellow → green, number counts up, error disappears) as a big close-up. Template `traffic_light.html`. |
| 8.6–11 s | **CTA on screen** | "Comment TOOL" as a bar over the tool, **no static end card**. That way the Reel loops seamlessly. |

**Caption:** repeat the hook (with the search term) → 2 lines of benefit → question for comments → **share prompt** ("Send this to the coworker who still …") → "Comment or DM TOOL" → compliance footer.

**Check after 48 h** (`06_analytics.md`): skip rate below 50 %? Average watch time above 70 % of the length? Shares ≥ saves? (Saves + shares) / reach ≥ 2 % → candidate for the paid boost.
