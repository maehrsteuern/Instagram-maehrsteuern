# 1 · Positioning, audiences, pillars

Source of truth: `00_us_brief.md`. This file explains the positioning in more depth and sets the KPIs.

## Core line
> **Tax × Code – tax know-how that computes.**
> I show tax teams how to replace fragile spreadsheets with AI and small tools that actually run – and build the solution for you if you want.

One sentence for every situation (bio, Reel end card, intro call):
"I help tax teams save hours with AI and small tools that actually run – and I'll build the solution for you if you want."

Who Loris is (always this wording, never more):
- "German-trained tax pro" / "trained in the German tax administration" (Diplom-Finanzwirt (FH), 3-year civil-service tax degree)
- "Certified AI Manager (IHK, German Chamber of Commerce)"
- Never "CPA", "EA", "tax attorney", "former IRS". No employer name, numbers or logos – demo data only.

The German angle is a hook, not a weakness:
> "I was trained by the German tax office – the most rule-obsessed tax system on earth. Now I build tools for US tax teams."

Why "Code" and not "AI" in the line: everyone says AI. The difference is software that runs, is auditable and survives the next deadline.

## Audiences – ranked
All four stay on board, but they have different jobs for the account:

| Rank | Group | Role for the account | What they want |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **In-house tax teams** (mid-market & corporate: tax provision / ASC 740, compliance, SOX) | Clients → demo + intro call | Fewer spreadsheet errors, audit trail, faster close |
| 2 | **CPA firms & tax preparers** (staff, seniors, managers, EAs) | Clients, referrals | Automate recurring calcs, survive busy season |
| 3 | **Small business owners, freelancers, creators** | Reach, occasional clients | Understand what happens with their taxes, simple calculators |
| 4 | **Accounting students & CPA candidates** | Reach, shares, community | Tax concepts that make sense, learning with code |

Rule: **every post serves one main group.** Client posts (rank 1–2) end with **"DM TOOL"**, reach posts (rank 3–4) end with **"Save this"** or **"Follow for more"**.

**Bridge topics "exam → practice"** (e.g. "What the CPA exam doesn't tell you: what a real provision workbook looks like") count as client posts (pillar 2/3): CPA candidates become staff and seniors within a year and are the warmest future clients.

**Starting from zero:** unlike the German sister account, @maehrtax has no existing community. Reach comes first from Reels (pillars 3–5), trust and leads from carousels and demos (pillars 1–2). Nothing from @maehrsteuern is cross-posted 1:1.

Weekly mix (5 Reels + 2 carousels, see `03_content_calendar.md`): roughly 2 × pillar 5, 1 × pillar 1, 1 × pillar 3 (number Reel), 1 × pillar 1/2/4 rotating; carousels alternate pillar 2 and 3. "Behind the code" at least every 2 weeks.

**"Behind the code" rule:** no tech jargon for the tax audience. Every look at code is **immediately tied to the aha moment in the tax team's day** ("These 10 lines mean you never hunt a #REF! again"), never code for code's sake. Programming terms (API, parser, framework …) only if translated in the same sentence.

## Formats (fixed, so you don't have to think every time)

**Reel (pillars 1, 3, 4, 5)**
- 6–25 s, hook in the first 1.5 s as on-screen text from frame 1
- Every Reel shows a **visible transformation** (chaos → clean, red → green, 2 hours → 17 seconds) and ends with a CTA
- Structure: pain (≤ 2 s) → turn (cut to the tool) → payoff (the satisfying moment) → CTA on screen (no static end card, so it loops)
- Cover from `templates/system/reel_cover.html`, text inside the center 3:4 area (profile grid)
- Demo data only. No real company names, logos or numbers.
- Details: `08_demo_reel_formula.md`, `09_reel_rules.md`, `10_software_reel_concept.md`, `16_us_growth_playbook.md`

**Carousel (pillars 2 and 3)**
- 1080 × 1350, 6–8 slides from `templates/system/carousel.html`
- Slide 1: promise with a number ("5 spreadsheet mistakes …") · slides 2–n: one idea per slide with a fix box · last slide: CTA
- Slide 2 must work on its own as an entry point (Instagram re-shows carousels starting at slide 2)
- Every carousel gets a companion Reel (same topic, one visible moment) that pulls new people to it

**Stories (daily, keep them small)**
- 9:00 AM ET: question, poll or quiz sticker on the topic of the day (`story.html`, type `question`), quiz answer the next morning
- Teaser "New post" 5 minutes after every carousel (type `teaser`)
- Good replies → screenshot in highlight "Feedback 🙏" (only with permission, names blacked out)

## Tone
- "You", short, concrete, one number per claim
- Jargon only with an example right behind it (ASC 740 → "the tax line in your financial statements")
- Never talk about the employer or real clients; demo company is "Northwind Manufacturing Inc." (fictional)
- Emojis sparingly: 🧠 ⚙️ 💬 ✅ – no emoji chains
- Educational posts end with the compliance footer: `Educational content – not tax, legal or accounting advice.`
