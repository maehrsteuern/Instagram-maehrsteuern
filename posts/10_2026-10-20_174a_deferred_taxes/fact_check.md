# Fact check – 10 § 174A: R&D expensing is back (deferred taxes)

**As of:** 2026-10-04 · **Post:** Tue 10/20/2026, 7:30 PM ET (carousel) + question story (10/19) + teaser

## Legal basis
- **One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA)**, Pub. L. 119-21, signed **July 4, 2025**, § 70302 ("full expensing of domestic research and experimental expenditures").
- New **IRC § 174A**: deduction for **domestic** research or experimental (R&E) expenditures paid or incurred in **tax years beginning after December 31, 2024**. Software development continues to count as R&E (verify wording in § 174A).
- Taxpayers may instead elect to capitalize and amortize domestic R&E (§ 174A(c), over not less than 60 months – verify).
- **IRC § 174** (as amended by TCJA, 2017) remains for **foreign** R&E: capitalize and amortize over **15 years** (mid-year convention). Domestic R&E in tax years beginning 2022–2024 had to be amortized over **5 years** (mid-year convention).
- **Transition rules (OBBBA § 70302(f))**:
  - All taxpayers: may elect to deduct the **remaining unamortized domestic R&E** from tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2021 and before Jan 1, 2025 **in full in the first tax year beginning after Dec 31, 2024**, or **ratably over the 2-year period** beginning with that year (for calendar-year taxpayers: 2025, or 2025 + 2026).
  - **Eligible small businesses** (not a tax shelter, § 448(c) gross receipts test met for the first tax year beginning after Dec 31, 2024 – $31M average) could elect to apply § 174A **retroactively** to tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2021 (amended returns).
- **Rev. Proc. 2025-28** (IRB 2025-38): procedures. Per secondary sources the small-business retroactive election had to be made on a timely filed original/superseded 2024 return or on amended returns filed **by July 6, 2026**. → By the post date (10/20/2026) this window has **closed**. Slide 6 therefore says "had a July 6, 2026 deadline – ask what still applies".
- Related, not on the slides: § 280C(c) research credit rules were changed by OBBBA as well (late § 280C(c)(2) election relief also July 6, 2026 per Rev. Proc. 2025-28).
- ASC 740: effects of a tax law change are recognized in the period that includes the enactment date (July 4, 2025 → Q3 2025 for calendar-year companies). ASC 730: R&D is generally expensed as incurred for book purposes.

## Demo math, step by step
1. Domestic R&E paid in 2025: **$2,000,000** (calendar-year C corporation, federal only, 21%).
2. Old § 174 (5-year amortization, mid-year convention): year 1 = $2,000,000 ÷ 5 × ½ = **$200,000** (10%). Years 2–5: $400,000 each; year 6: $200,000.
3. New § 174A: deduct **$2,000,000** in 2025.
4. Extra deduction in year 1: $2,000,000 − $200,000 = **$1,800,000**.
5. Federal tax effect year 1: $1,800,000 × 21% = **$378,000** less federal tax.
6. Deferred taxes (illustration, not on the slides): under the old rule the company would have booked a DTA of $1,800,000 × 21% = $378,000 at the end of 2025 (book basis 0, tax basis $1.8M). Under § 174A there is no temporary difference → no DTA, but $378,000 lower current tax. Total tax expense is the same – it just shifts from deferred to current.
