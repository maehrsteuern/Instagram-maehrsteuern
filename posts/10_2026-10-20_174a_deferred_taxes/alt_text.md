# Alt text – 10 § 174A deferred taxes (one line per slide)
1. Dark green slide, headline: "§ 174A: R&D expensing is back." Subline: same $2M of R&D, +$378,000 less federal tax in year 1, and deferred taxes move.
2. "What changed": in 2022–2024, research and experimental costs had to be capitalized (5 years domestic, 15 years foreign). The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed July 4, 2025, added § 174A: domestic R&E is deductible again for tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2024; foreign R&E stays at 15 years.
3. Table "The example" for $2.0M domestic R&E in 2025: old § 174 year-1 deduction 10% = $200,000; new § 174A deduction $2,000,000; extra deduction in year 1 +$1,800,000.
4. "What it's worth": $1,800,000 times 21% equals $378,000 less federal tax in year 1. Note: timing, not a permanent saving.
5. "And your deferred taxes?": 2022–2024 capitalization created a deferred tax asset. New domestic R&E creates no new DTA; the old balance reverses as it is deducted; total tax expense barely moves – it shifts from deferred to current.
6. "Options to check with your advisor": keep amortizing the 2022–2024 balance, accelerate it into 2025 or 2025–2026, and a note that the small-business retroactive option for 2022–2024 had a July 6, 2026 deadline – ask your advisor what still applies.
