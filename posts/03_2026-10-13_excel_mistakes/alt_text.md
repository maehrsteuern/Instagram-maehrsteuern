# Alt text – 03 spreadsheet mistakes (one line per slide)
1. Dark green slide, headline: "5 spreadsheet mistakes in your tax provision" – and the fix for each one.
2. Mistake 01, "Hard-coded 21%": the rate typed into 400 cells goes stale when a rate changes. Fix: one rate table as a parameter that every cell points to.
3. Mistake 02, "Broken links → #REF!": the provision pulls from a moved file called Provision_Final_v3_NEW.xlsx. Fix: one data source, import instead of linking files.
4. Mistake 03, "No version history": nobody knows who changed row 48 or why. Fix: an automatic change log with date, person and reason.
5. Mistake 04, "No audit trail": the SOX auditor will ask where a number comes from. Fix: every number traces back to its source in one click.
6. Mistake 05, "Rounding too early": rounding each line creates $1 differences fixed by hand. Fix: calculate at full precision and round once at the end.
