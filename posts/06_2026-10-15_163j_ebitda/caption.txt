§ 163(j) is back on EBITDA – your interest limit just grew.

Demo company: $14.0M EBITDA, $4.0M D&A, $4.0M business interest.
Old rule (EBIT): 30% × $10.0M = $3.0M limit → $1.0M disallowed.
New rule (EBITDA, tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2024): 30% × $14.0M = $4.2M → all $4.0M deductible.
