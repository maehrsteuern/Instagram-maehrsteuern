# Alt text – 06 § 163(j) EBITDA (one line per slide)
1. Dark green slide, headline: "§ 163(j) is back on EBITDA." Subline: same company, same debt, +$1,000,000 more interest deduction.
2. "What changed": the business interest limit is 30% of adjusted taxable income (ATI). The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed July 4, 2025, adds depreciation and amortization back. 2022–2024: ATI roughly EBIT; tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2024: roughly EBITDA. Simplified.
3. Table "Our demo company" (fictional, 2025): EBITDA $14,000,000; depreciation and amortization $4,000,000; EBIT $10,000,000; business interest expense $4,000,000.
4. Table "Before vs. after": ATI $10.0M vs. $14.0M; limit at 30% $3.0M vs. $4.2M; interest $4.0M in both; disallowed $1.0M vs. $0; deductible $3.0M vs. $4.0M.
5. "What it's worth": extra deduction $1,000,000 times 21% federal rate equals $210,000 less federal tax. Note: mostly timing, because disallowed interest carries forward.
6. "What to check in your 2025 numbers": ATI recomputed with D&A, prior-year disallowed interest carryforward used, deferred tax asset updated, small business exemption, state conformity.
